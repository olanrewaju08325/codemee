# CodeMe Academy Lesson Authoring Guide

## Rich Content Editor
Lessons support markdown-style rich formatting to ensure a professional presentation.

### Best Practices
- **Objectives:** Clearly define 2-3 Learning Objectives per lesson.
- **Estimated Duration:** Always assign an estimated duration to inform student pacing.
- **AI Context:** Provide clear, explicit text within the lesson content. The embedded AI Tutor utilizes the lesson's text and objectives as its primary contextual anchor when answering student questions.
- **Resources:** Limit attachments per lesson to strictly relevant materials to avoid overwhelming students.
